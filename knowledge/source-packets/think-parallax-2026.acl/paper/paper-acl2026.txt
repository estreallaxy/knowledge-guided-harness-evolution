Figure 2: The architecture of ParallaxRAG, illustrating its three main stages: Preprocess, Retrieval, and Generate.
The core retrieval process begins by creating specialized multi-view representations for both the graph (b) and the
question (c), a process guided by directional distance encoding and regularized by pairwise similarity (a). Next,
candidate triples are scored in parallel across these diverse views (d). Finally, a query-aware gating mechanism
computes head importance (e) to produce a weighted aggregation of the scores, identifying the most relevant triples
for the final generation stage.
