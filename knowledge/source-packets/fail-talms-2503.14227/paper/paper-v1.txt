In §3.4 we propose to use model-based evaluation
to examine the correctness of model-generated re-
sponses, i.e., pass rate. To validate the reliabil-
ity of GPT-4o’s pass rate evaluation, we analyzed
95 examples randomly sampled from GPT-4o’s re-
sponses on the perfect setting and compare the pass
rate results between human evaluators and GPT-4o.
The comparison showed an 83.2% agreement in
the correctness of model-generated final responses
