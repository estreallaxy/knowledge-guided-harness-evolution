# liYK source mechanisms: parameter-training boundary review

Historical review date: 2026-10-13. This record evaluates selected mechanisms with claim mappings against the no-training integration requirement added in `b638c977`; it does not guarantee that an entire paper or repository is training-free. Codex performed static review of the paper text and pinned source. Team human review, full dependency execution, and H0 transfer remain incomplete.

Historical scope is eight packets: the original six-packet batch included NORA, followed by G-Memory and AgentDojo additions. It covers 27 selected mechanism/boundary claims plus two excluded paper-result claims (29 total), and is not a new seven-source result for the 2026-10-26 PR. The current six base sources are ReasoningBank, ExpeL, AgentRewind, StateFork, AgentScope, and AgentDojo, with G-Memory as a memory supplement. AgentDojo's historical addition status does not change its current base role. New NORA changes are excluded from that PR; historical NORA evidence may refer to material already on main.

These conclusions cover 27 selected mechanism/boundary claims. ReasoningBank RB5 reports paper system-level numbers; NORA NO4 provides qualitative paper evidence from removing hooks. Both retain their original conclusions but are excluded from judgments about independent mechanisms, parameter training, or individual mechanism gains. On 2026-10-13 an independent agent identified the previous erroneous inclusion of NO4; the count was corrected from 28 to 27. This does not establish local installability, absence of additional model services, zero cost, complete reproduction, or effective transfer. Other newly indexed sources require separate verification. Original files and historical conclusions are retained; no local file checksums are computed or saved.
